# Just Trees: developer notes

What the game is, where everything lives, how to build and test it, the design decisions to keep, and what's next. Section 6 is a short changelog.

**The idea:** a map-making battle/hunting game — a brand-new map every game, balanced, endless variety; no progression or leaderboards. It stays on the web: free, one HTML file. Priorities: map variety and quality, fair maps, smooth frame rate, reliable online play. **Art direction:** free artist-made models (Quaternius, CC0); anything made from scratch matches that faceted low-poly, flat-colour style. Players must be **21+** (birth-year check).

## 1. Where things are

- **Repo:** `github.com/JustTrees/JUST-TREES`, branch `main` (push prints "repository moved": fine). **Live:** https://justtrees.github.io/JUST-TREES/ (1–2 min after a push; the menu shows the version). Also served by the Cloudflare Worker https://just-trees-online.ron-bb2.workers.dev (builds from main on every push).
- **`index.html`** (~1.6 MB, one file): Three.js r149 and PeerJS 1.5.4 inlined at the top, then the game script. The script starts with a **table of contents** (search words per part) and a **tuning block** after `VERSION` (guns `WEAP`, `TIER` stars, `NADE`, `LOOT`, `PICK`, PvP timings, zone/capture). About 1.2 MB of it is built-in textures and sounds: they stay inline on purpose so a backup file opened from disk still works.
- **Loaded on demand** (over http only; a copy opened from disk falls back to the old hand-built versions):
  - `models/guns/<kind>_<tier>.glb` — 20 pack guns (1.89). `models/bows/bow_<2-5>.json` — pack bows (2.04).
  - `models/dinos/*.bin` — 6 pack dinosaurs + the from-scratch pterosaur (QDN1 format). `models/animals/*.bin` — every hunting animal (2.03).
  - `sounds/trex/`, `sounds/dino/`, `sounds/beast/` — dinosaur recordings.
- **`models/source/`** — the original model and sound packs (see its README for licences). **Keep them all.** Used so far: Ultimate Gun Pack (guns), Dinosaur Animated Pack (dinos), Farm Animals (all animals), Medieval Weapons Pack (bows), the CC0 sound files. **Saved for later, unused:** Stylized Nature MegaKit (trees/rocks/plants), Medieval Village MegaKit (house pieces), Farm Buildings, Fish Pack (fish, dolphin, manta, shark, whale), Monster Pack (bat, dragon, skeleton, slime), the rest of the Medieval Weapons Pack (swords, axes, shields), the pack arrow. Not kept in the repo (licence: no redistribution): QAL packs, the Ririsaurus Dinosaurs/Beasts sound packs (only processed sounds are in `sounds/`).
- **`dev/`** — tools and tests (section 5), plus the model pipelines: `dev/guns/build_guns.py`, `dev/dinos/exp.*` + `make_ptero.py`, `dev/animals/` (dump, `animal_lib.py`, `build_animals.py`, `build_rigged.py`, `view.py`), `dev/bows/make_bows.py`.
- **Cloudflare:** `cf/worker.js` serves the site and answers `/turn` with a 6-hour Cloudflare TURN pass for justtrees.github.io / the workers.dev address / localhost. Secrets `TURN_KEY_ID`, `TURN_KEY_TOKEN` are Runtime secrets set in the Cloudflare dashboard. Also `/rooms` (open games, DO `Rooms`), `/count` (maps per day, DO `Counts`) and `/dev` (Dev Mode saves, DO `DevStore`, 2.45: saving needs the `DEV_KEY` secret, set in the dashboard like the TURN ones and never written anywhere else; reading is open; 2.46: each save is kept in 100 kB pieces under `kind:NAME#i` with a small record `kind:NAME` = {name, t, n, pub, info}, up to 1.5 MB; the list returns name, time, published, info). `.assetsignore` keeps dev/, cf/, models/source, HANDOFF.md off the site. Code goes live by pushing to GitHub (the Worker builds from `main`).

## 2. The game now (v2.52)

### Modes
- **Hiking** (no guns, no fog; **in a headset, solo or online, the map is Island = the 3 km XXXL island, or Endless** — 2.19, `set.hikeMap`, `HIKEMAP`; PC/phone hiking keeps the normal sizes), **Hunting** (animals; VR has loot guns, bow, grenades; Prehistoric switch = dinosaurs), **PvP** (VR: solo against bots, or online with bots filling the room; PC/phone have the old bots version, on hold). **Online works in every mode** (2.00).

### World
- **Settings:** latitude (climate), roughness, time of day, month (season), tree density, map size (XS 250 m, S 400, M 560, L 800, XL 1.2 km, XXL 1.6 km, XXXL 3 km — XXXL not on phones), edges (Island / Endless), landscape (Natural / Dramatic), weather.
- **Environments by latitude:** `climateOf(lat)` bands (tropical <23°, temperate <50, boreal <62, tundra <72, polar); `envOptions(lat)` lists what occurs there with weights; `rollEnv()` picks one per map from the seed (`MAPENV`); `biomeOf(lat)` returns it. Environments: rainforest, palm jungle, tropical, desert, badlands (red rock country), swamp, temperate forest, birch-maple, redwood (redwoods, sequoias, eucalyptus), boreal, tundra, polar, plus the equator **archipelago** (Island + 0°, `ARCHI`, ~40% roll). `TROPIC(b)`, `DRY(b)`, `TB(b)` (temperate/boreal tables for look-alike biomes). **A new environment must be added to every per-biome table** (`GRASS`, `WATERC`, `SKYTOP`, water fraction, snow/tree lines, `dens`, rock tone/moss, logs, bushes, clouds, haze, weather, flocks, `NATIVE`) and to `envOptions` — or use `TB`.
- **Terrain:** heightfield with erosion; `H(x,z)` is the surface. Dramatic maps get one landmark (`makeFeature`: canyons, rifts, cliffs, mesas, buttes, craters, volcanoes…; on XXXL `BIG` landforms ~half the map and mountain ranges). Volcano craters can hold lava (`LAVA`, `inLava`; stepping in = respawn).
- **Trees:** trunk + branch cards in Pop: One style; fluffy forked crowns for some broadleafs (`FLUFFY`, patchy by `fluffPatch`), old pines, aspens, birches, maples, redwoods, sequoias, eucalyptus, cactus, Joshua trees, junipers, palms, cypress. Three distances with fades (`FADE_D`: detail → simple 3D → flat cards facing you; `NEAR` batches, `CARD` sheet baked per map). **The giant tree** (one per map, `PORCH`): a porch ~⅔ up with the only red 5★ sniper.
- **Endless (2.19, VR hiking):** land built in 200 m squares (`EN_C`) round you, a little each frame (`endlessTick`, ~3 ms budget in a headset, less if the frame rate drops; 11 ms on menus), squares past ~1.4 km let go (`enUnload`). Everything in a square comes from the seed + its position (`enHash` of its wrapped index), so a spot is always the same, for everyone online too; **the world repeats every 25 km** (`EN_P`; `makePNoise` = noise that repeats; tree/colour patterns read at `enWrap(x)`). Pieces: `enWorld` (the island's `buildHeight` made periodic + swamp/desert/badlands touches; **no droplet erosion yet**), `enHeightsGen` (4 m grid + 8-cell border, summit weathering, borders shared with neighbours so seams are exact), `ENDLESS.make` (inside `generate`, so it uses the same `treeAt`, `pickVar`, `groundCol`, rock/log/bush rules as the island maps, scaled per square by `AREA`), near batches that grow (`enSlot`/`enNearTick`, 100 m cells), cards per square, tree shadows in a 3.2 km wrap-round picture stamped per square once all 8 neighbours exist (`enShadowGen`), solid rocks only within ~430 m (`enSolidGen`, per-square 1 m grids; `rockTopAt` branch), water plane + shallows texture following you (`uShoreC`), a coarse far-ground sheet under everything so nothing is missing at the haze (`enFarJob`/`enFarSink`), the hand map as a 1.6 km window (`MAPV`, explored 25 m squares in `ENDLESS.seen`). Start: the squares within ~430 m are built in `generate` (2.5–4 s on a PC); the rest grows during the little-map screen; dropping in builds the squares round your spot behind the fade (`enEnsureHere`). Online: a friend's pose is shifted by whole loops (`EN_P`). No giant tree / porch yet. Test: `dev/test_endless.py` (seams, walk keeps up, revisit + loop give the same land, drop-in).
- **Endless regions (2.20):** the land is split into regions ~2.5 km across (`EN_RC`, warped Voronoi `enRegions` → up to 3 weights in `EN_RL`, blended over a wide border `EN_BL` = 900, ~800 m you can see changing; 2.21, was 380). Each region (`enRegInfo`, cached by wrapped cell) gets any of the 11 environments at random (or the menu's one if a specific environment is chosen), its own roughness (`set.rough` ± .5), its own weather (`weatherFor`, when the menu's weather is Random) and sky. Each environment uses its own typical latitude (`EN_LAT`) for the menu month. The per-environment rules now live in module-level `function* envGen(o)` (snow/tree lines, ground colours, tree kinds, `treeAt`); the island maps call it once (verified identical with same.py), endless makes a light one per environment at start (`E.envL`) and the heavy one (tree kinds, cards, limbs) the first time it is needed, a slice at a time, prefetched on rings ahead of you (`enPrepGen`, `E.prepQ`; tree kinds that look the same are shared via `E.kindCache`). In a square (`enMake` in en_make), each tree/rock/bush spot takes the environment of its spot (random by the blend weights near borders, so stands mix). Heights: `E.hNF` blends per-environment base heights (percentile-matched for each roughness, + swamp/desert/badlands touches), `E.hW` adds features. **Features:** one chance per 3.125 km square (`EN_FC`, `enFeat`: 85% with Dramatic, 40% with Natural), made by the island's `makeFeature` at the 3 km map's size (volcano, crater, canyon, butte, range, sink, terrace, rift…). Volcanoes get lava (`enLavaGen`, `enLavaAt`, `lavaAt`). As you walk `enAmbient` blends fog/light/sky/water colours and picks the rain/snow of the strongest wet region (strength = its share, `weather.pk`), and sets footsteps, sounds and the map's environment.
- **Endless 2.21:** **a day in 24 minutes** (`E.clock`, `hourNow` += dt/60 in `endlessTick` while playing, from the menu hour; `enLight` relights every 0.5 s; host sends `{t:"hr"}` every 4 s and in the start message, guests ease to it, `enClockSet`). **Tree shadows turn with the sun:** when it has moved ~5° (`enShadowSun`), the squares within 800 m (`EN_SHR`) are painted again into a second picture (`Sh.spare`/`Sh.nx`, `enShadowRebake`, `enShadowGen(ch, T)`), farther ones cleared, then it fades in (`uShadowMix`) and the two swap; no moon shadows on endless (they'd point the wrong way). **Far tree cards** are painted in a fixed light (`CARD_STD`) and tinted live (`CARD_TINT`, uniform `uCardTint` in `cardMaterial`), so they darken at dusk with the near trees. **Hand map** has its own small lane each frame (`E.mapJob`, 0.7 ms; recentres when you're 240 m off) — before, building new land starved it while walking. **Marks/bullets/arrows/grenades/lightning** use `outMap()` (no island edge on endless) — before, marks failed past 1.6 km from the start. **Leaving environments behind:** `enEnvGC` (in the 0.4 s scan) frees an environment unused for 60 s by any loaded square, the prep queue or the look-ahead ring (`enEnvFree`: slots, geometries, materials, textures, card cells → `E.sheet.free`); tree kinds shared with a live environment stay. **Env prep in smaller steps** (each atlas, odd/fluffy shape, bush, limb), and `enPrepGen` keeps the environment's own random stream across steps (before, shapes after the first step drew from the global stream, so they could differ between players). **Joining an endless hike online** puts you ~10 m from the host (before: back at the start). Tests: `test_endless` (map lag, far mark, clock, sun turns, same shapes), `test_en_online` (join near host, shared clock, split 3 km).
- **Endless animals (2.22, hiking):** the land is cut into 500 m squares (`EA_C`, 50 per loop). When a square comes within ~850 m of a player (`EA_ON`), its ground animals are planned from a seeded stream per wrapped square (`enAnimFill`: 3–5 groups, each kind picked from `NATIVE` of the environment where the group stands, rabbits weighted down; spots via `enAnimSpot` — water's edge for herons/gators/polar bears, high ground for goats/bighorn, shore for seals) and made a few per frame from `E.aQ` (≤1 ms; never within 700 m of anyone — they're out beyond the 750 m animal draw distance; dropping in fills behind the fade, `enEnsureHere` → `E.aInit`). Animals >1.25 km from everyone are taken away (`enAnimDrop`: rig meshes freed, shared model shapes kept); a square forgets itself once everyone is 1.6 km off, so it fills again later. Birds: one flock or lone bird per square ~3 in 4, made on each headset for itself (`E.acB`, `enBirdFill`). Coats follow the region (`addGround` uses the region's biome/season on endless). Online: endless counts like hunting for animals (`mpNetGuest`, `beastThreat`, `mpBeastSend`) — the host runs ground animals round every player and sends each friend the ones within 480 m. Same wandering/fleeing as hunting (`updateAnimals`, `appearTick`). No legends (Bigfoot/Yeti) or monkeys (need perch trees) on endless yet. Test: `dev/test_en_animals.py`; online part in `test_en_online`.
- **Endless Hunting (2.23, headset, solo and online):** the setup's Map size row has an **Endless** button in Hunting (`set.huntMap`, sent online; `huntEndless()`; the Edges buttons hide). `HIKEMAP` is "endless" for it too, so everything endless applies (regions, clock, features). `enAnimInit` starts hunting as `spawnAnimals` does (score, `DINO`). Ground fog for the start weather. Tests: `dev/test_en_hunt.py`, Prehistoric online in `test_en_online`.

### Animals (Hunting)
- **Kinds per biome** in `NATIVE` (42 kinds incl. Bigfoot/Yeti); spawn plan in `spawnAnimals`, built by `addGround` / `spawnKind`. Habitat liking (`PREF`, `habInit`, home ranges), whole-map stocking in ~200 m squares (`STOCK`, `stockTick`), `appearTick` brings groups in out of sight when you stand still, XXXL only within 1 km (`BUBBLE`). They notice you walking at 60 m, sprinting 150 m; a shot empties the area (`scatterBeasts`, `fleeFar`). Score = distance × `SCORE[kind]`; a hit is a kill.
- **Models (2.03):** every animal is still built by its old hand-built builder first (`buildQuad`+`QSPEC`, `buildBuck`, `buildBear`, `buildWolf`, `buildApe`, `buildBird`, `buildMonkey`, …) — **kept as hidden rigs, don't delete**. Then:
  - `QA_KINDS` (four-legged, from the farm pack): `qaPick` sets `A.qf` (file) and `A.qv` (coat: horse ×5, bear black, panther black in the tropics, wolf/rabbit pale/white in the cold, arctic fox grey out of the cold, crocodile = gator coat 1, half the lions `lion_m` with a mane); `qdAttach` fits the model to the old body's length and hides it; `qdTick` picks Idle / Eat (grazing) / Walk / Run / Death, leans with slopes, updates far ones less often (4 fps past 320 m unless scoped). Hit spheres fitted per bone (`qdSpheres`, `A.qhit`; Head bone = head shot).
  - `QR_KINDS` (birds, turkey, roadrunner, heron, seal, monkey, Bigfoot, Yeti; built from scratch): `qrAttach` skins the model onto the old animal's moving groups (bone names = slots: body, head, leg{i}, leg{i}k, arm{i}, arm{i}k, wing{i}), so the game's own flapping/striding/swinging drives it; old hit boxes stay. Birds share one shape with colour slots filled from `BIRD_COL`/`PARROT_COL` (`qdGeo`).
  - Rebuild: `python3 dev/animals/build_animals.py <dumps> models/animals` (dumps made by `dev/animals/dump.py` from the Farm Animals FBX) and `python3 dev/animals/build_rigged.py models/animals`; look with `dev/animals/view.py`.
- **Legends:** Bigfoot (Yeti in the cold), Large maps and up, hidden and unhittable until you hold the red sniper ("There's a Bigfoot in these woods"); notices you through max-zoom binoculars; one real shot, then he sprints off and is gone.
- Online guests get the host's animals (`an` messages, `mpNetBeast`); coats are rolled per player (colours can differ).

### Prehistoric (VR Hunting, `set.dino`)
- Herds of triceratops, parasaur, stego, bronto (`DINO_NATIVE`), sizes vary (`DINO_GROW`); health per kind (`DINO_HP`), damage per gun (`DINO_DMG`, `RAPTOR_DMG` × stars; head ×1.5); no health bar.
- **Raptor hunts** (`dinoTick`, `huntTick`): random times, warning cue, circles far and fast, feints, heals unless hit by the sniper, charges at ≤30%, inside 15 m it gets you (red blackout, respawn).
- **T. rex** (Large+: 1/2/3/4 by size; `rexTick`): revealed by the red sniper (scream across the map), roams and screams every 30–60 s; shot → chases in wide S-curves with screaming stops; 3500 HP; a climb over 6 m saves you.
- **2.50 hunts:** while a T. rex is after you (`rexChase`: any `rexOn` T. rex within 400 m in Hunting) climbing is off: `climbTick` lets go of holds with no fling, banner "T. rex! No climbing · RUN". The chase runs at `max(run, 10.5)` m/s (a little under a headset sprint) on wider curves. More than 100 m out after 6 s, `rexCallPartner` brings ONE extra T. rex (`B.extra`, `A.pair`/`B.leader`) 110–150 m ahead of where you're running, out of view; giving up ends both and the extra walks off and is removed past 160 m. A T. rex hunt sends the raptors off. **Raptor packs** (`dinoSpawnHunter`): 30% two, 15% three (`DINO.pack`; `DINO.packK = 0` gives lone raptors for the older tests); each runs `huntTick` with its own ring (`ringK` .8/1.2) and opposite orbit, charges are 1.6 s apart (`DINO.lastCharge`) and the others call; when the leader goes the next one carries on; a bite sends the rest off. Test: test_250.
- **Pterosaurs** (2.02, `PT`, `pteroTick`): circle 45–80 m up, pass under one (or shoot it) → screech and dive; reaching you = blackout.
- Models: Quaternius dinosaurs (`models/dinos`, `dev/dinos/exp.py`), from-scratch pterosaur (`make_ptero.py`). **Skins (2.16):** a scaly pattern over the plain colours (`dinoSkinOn`, `DINO_SKIN`: the 1.82 `dinoSkin` Voronoi scales, big for the large ones, small for the raptor; a leathery `leatherSkin` for the pterosaur), projected three ways in the model's own space (no UVs needed; rides with the bones), fades out 45–70 m away (`SKIN.fade`); T. rex a bit darker green (`DINO_RECOL`). 2.17: the hunting animals too (`ANIMAL_SKIN`, `SKIN.animal`): fine short fur in soft clumps (`furSkin`), wrinkled hide on elephant / rhino, scales on gator / crocodile, overlapping feathers (`featherSkin`) on the birds (all birds share `bird`), turkey, roadrunner, heron. Per-pattern strength `SKIN.amt`; an entry's 3rd number scales it (seal, tapir, panther lighter). It multiplies the coat, so near-black coats (black bear, Bigfoot) barely show it. Sounds: `DINO_SFX` / `DINO_RATE` (recordings pitched per animal, `name@rate`), 3D voices with forest reverb, one limiter; made-up fallbacks.

### VR

## 3. How to build and test

- **Edit with a Python patch script** in the scratchpad that replaces exact strings in a copy of `index.html`, **asserting each old string's count**; bump `VERSION` and `<title>`; build to the scratchpad, copy to `index.html` when tests pass.
- **Tools in `dev/`** (Playwright + Chromium/SwiftShader):
  - `check_code.py [file]`: parses, no duplicate function names, no debug hooks.
  - `make_debug.py index.html dbg.html`: debug copy with `window.FREEZE` (step frames by hand) and `window.D` (handles into the game; add more there when a test needs them).
  - `run_all.py <file> --nm path/to/node_modules [--jobs 3] [--only test_a,test_b] [--full]`: code check + tests. **Quick by default (2.48):** every test runs, but the slow ones do less of the same thing (they read `JT_QUICK=1`: test_landscape one small island size and no pictures, test_solid_ground one forest and 900 rays, test_porch 2 maps, test_spread 2 maps, test_3km no dinosaur map, test_en_hunt / test_en_animals / test_endless shorter walks, test_cards the medium map only, test_load one mode, test_242 / test_239 skip drawing their pictures) and no pictures are saved; **`--full`** = everything with pictures (big releases, or to dig into a failure). A test that fails gets one more go on its own at the end (busy-machine timeouts). The slowest tests start first (last run's times are kept in the temp folder) and the online ones run one at a time beside the rest. Run it in the background (Bash `run_in_background`) and read the log. Online tests (test_mp, test_mp_fail, test_lobby, test_online, test_en_online, test_rooms, test_243, test_246, test_248) need that node_modules path (run_all starts its own local PeerJS server). For a small change, `--only` with the tests that touch it is enough.
  - **Most tests step frames by hand** (`window.FREEZE`), so a crash in the real frame loop (`loop()`) slips past them: `test_249` runs the real loop through the menus; add stages to it when a stage changes something every frame relies on (fog, lights).
  - Tests that load models serve the page over http (test_qdino, test_qanimals, test_bowtiers, test_rexsound); the rest open it from disk (old hand-built models).
  - Flaky under heavy parallel load, pass alone: test_dino (random raptor moods), test_habitat, test_spread, test_trex, test_bow. The model-loading tests (test_qdino, test_qanimals, test_bowtiers) and the big-map ones (test_landscape, test_solid_ground, test_cards) time out with `--jobs 5`: rerun them with `--jobs 1`–`2`.
- **Push:** `git add`, commit, `git push origin main`; then send the backup file.

### Rules
- **Never ship the debug build:** `index.html` must not contain `window.D=`, `window.FREEZE`, `window.__trees`, `tilesX`, `cullTilesX`, `kindsX`, `window.__ah` (`dev/check_code.py` checks some; check the rest before copying).
- **Never put secrets** (TURN keys etc.) in the repo or the game.
- **Keep PC and phone unchanged** unless a change is meant for them; most work is **VR only**.
- **Keep every model/sound pack** in `models/source` (the unused ones are for future upgrades; each file under 100 MB).
- **Testing:** while building, run the tests for what you touched; run the full suite once before pushing. The slow/flaky ones (test_bow, test_bowtiers, test_psound, test_qanimals, test_qdino, test_dino) usually pass when rerun alone.
- **Each version:** bump `VERSION`, build, test, push to `main`. Commits use the JustTrees account's GitHub no-reply address.

### Gotchas (don't relearn these)
- In headless Chrome on this 2-core machine, endless walks show occasional 30–130 ms pauses even in trivial steps: garbage collection, not our steps (they were there in 2.19/2.20 too). If the Quest hitches, look at allocation churn (per-square Float32Arrays, `enBlock`, Colors in `setLighting`).
- Endless: anything scaled by height-over-water ÷ `span` must be capped — the big features stand many spans high (2.20: rock sections grew to 100 m). In picture scripts the sky dome only follows the camera in the live loop; move it yourself or you see a grey dome.
- Renderer outputs linear colour: **don't mark canvas textures as sRGB** or menus go dark. Hex colours in the model builders are sRGB → linear × `LIFT` (2.3).
- **Inside a VR session `requestAnimationFrame` doesn't run** — everything goes through `renderer.setAnimationLoop(loop)`.
- VR movement: `vr.readInputs()` gives world-space poses; walking moves the play space (`vr.ox/oy/oz/yaw`); `vr.placeAt` re-anchors on a new map. `VR_ON` must be set before `generate()`.
- `obstacles` is replaced by every `generate()`: use a getter in debug hooks.
- Two functions with the same name: the later silently wins (`check_code.py` catches it). A `//` comment in a one-line statement swallows the rest of the line — use `/* */` there.
- `vr.cl` is always an object in VR: test `vr.cl && vr.cl.hold` for "holding on".
- Call `updateMatrixWorld` before raycasts if things moved this frame.
- Quaternius farm rig: the feet are IK targets under the root, not children of the legs (`animal_lib` snaps them to the leg ends).
- A material's `onBeforeCompile` text isn't part of three's program cache key: anything that differs between materials sharing a hook must be a uniform, not baked into the shader text (`customProgramCacheKey` when the code differs).
- Island maps must stay identical when generation code is shared: compare a content fingerprint (trees, rocks, heights) old vs new with seeded `Math.random`, not pixels (rendering varies run to run).
- In the shell, `pkill -f` / `pgrep -f` can match the running shell and kill it; avoid them. Use the Bash timeout (up to 10 min) or nohup for long runs.

## 4. Design decisions (don't undo these)

- **No resource gathering or building.** Fights are about aim, movement and terrain.
- No camera bob; slow soft footsteps; quiet wind/trees/rain. No light shafts. No tree sway.
- Rounded peaks, no spikes or monoliths. Long views (cut far detail before view distance). Hiking has no fog.
- Native animals only; hit = kill with a bing, no blood; score per metre; animals react to your movement, not sound. Dinosaurs: no health bar; scary is good.
- VR: no jump/crouch buttons; off hand never steers the aim; snap turn 30°; heavy gun smoothing; two-handed scoping; binoculars at your face; glide with arms out; Pop: One climbing (high gravity, medium fling); unlimited ammo; recoil only on the auto guns (2.08).
- Avatars: **VR (2.37): other players and PvP bots are people** (see the 2.37 line; character decisions there). PC/phone: soldiers in baggy camo with hoods; your own VR body is your character (2.39); masks are achievements only. Online: no gamer tags over heads, no armbands (talk to know who's who), lobby: a picture card per player (2.38).
- Battle royale is shelved.
- Map making stays a dev tool (Map Lab): players play the published arenas but don't build or publish maps themselves.
