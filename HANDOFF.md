## 4. The game now (v2.37)

### Modes
- **Hiking** (no guns, no fog; **in a headset, solo or online, the map is Island = the 3 km XXXL island, or Endless** — 2.19, `set.hikeMap`, `HIKEMAP`; PC/phone hiking keeps the normal sizes), **Hunting** (animals; VR has loot guns, bow, grenades; Prehistoric switch = dinosaurs), **PvP** (VR online; PC/phone have the old bots version, on hold). **Online works in every mode** (2.00).

### World
- **Settings:** latitude (climate), roughness, time of day, month (season), tree density, map size (XS 250 m, S 400, M 560, L 800, XL 1.2 km, XXL 1.6 km, XXXL 3 km — XXXL not on phones), edges (Island / Endless), landscape (Natural / Dramatic), weather.
- **Environments by latitude:** `climateOf(lat)` bands (tropical <23°, temperate <50, boreal <62, tundra <72, polar); `envOptions(lat)` lists what occurs there with weights; `rollEnv()` picks one per map from the seed (`MAPENV`); `biomeOf(lat)` returns it. Environments: rainforest, palm jungle, tropical, desert, badlands (red rock country), swamp, temperate forest, birch-maple, redwood (redwoods, sequoias, eucalyptus), boreal, tundra, polar, plus the equator **archipelago** (Island + 0°, `ARCHI`, ~40% roll). `TROPIC(b)`, `DRY(b)`, `TB(b)` (temperate/boreal tables for look-alike biomes). **A new environment must be added to every per-biome table** (`GRASS`, `WATERC`, `SKYTOP`, water fraction, snow/tree lines, `dens`, rock tone/moss, logs, bushes, clouds, haze, weather, flocks, `NATIVE`) and to `envOptions` — or use `TB`.
- **Terrain:** heightfield with erosion; `H(x,z)` is the surface. Dramatic maps get one landmark (`makeFeature`: canyons, rifts, cliffs, mesas, buttes, craters, volcanoes…; on XXXL `BIG` landforms ~half the map and mountain ranges). Volcano craters can hold lava (`LAVA`, `inLava`; stepping in = respawn).
- **Trees:** trunk + branch cards in Pop: One style; fluffy forked crowns for some broadleafs (`FLUFFY`, patchy by `fluffPatch`), old pines, aspens, birches, maples, redwoods, sequoias, eucalyptus, cactus, Joshua trees, junipers, palms, cypress. Three distances with fades (`FADE_D`: detail → simple 3D → flat cards facing you; `NEAR` batches, `CARD` sheet baked per map). **The giant tree** (one per map, `PORCH`): a porch ~⅔ up with the only red 5★ sniper.
- **Endless (2.19, VR hiking):** land built in 200 m squares (`EN_C`) round you, a little each frame (`endlessTick`, ~3 ms budget in a headset, less if the frame rate drops; 11 ms on menus), squares past ~1.4 km let go (`enUnload`). Everything in a square comes from the seed + its position (`enHash` of its wrapped index), so a spot is always the same, for everyone online too; **the world repeats every 25 km** (`EN_P`; `makePNoise` = noise that repeats; tree/colour patterns read at `enWrap(x)`). Pieces: `enWorld` (the island's `buildHeight` made periodic + swamp/desert/badlands touches; **no droplet erosion yet**), `enHeightsGen` (4 m grid + 8-cell border, summit weathering, borders shared with neighbours so seams are exact), `ENDLESS.make` (inside `generate`, so it uses the same `treeAt`, `pickVar`, `groundCol`, rock/log/bush rules as the island maps, scaled per square by `AREA`), near batches that grow (`enSlot`/`enNearTick`, 100 m cells), cards per square, tree shadows in a 3.2 km wrap-round picture stamped per square once all 8 neighbours exist (`enShadowGen`), solid rocks only within ~430 m (`enSolidGen`, per-square 1 m grids; `rockTopAt` branch), water plane + shallows texture following you (`uShoreC`), a coarse far-ground sheet under everything so nothing is missing at the haze (`enFarJob`/`enFarSink`), the hand map as a 1.6 km window (`MAPV`, explored 25 m squares in `ENDLESS.seen`). Start: the squares within ~430 m are built in `generate` (2.5–4 s on a PC); the rest grows during the little-map screen; dropping in builds the squares round your spot behind the fade (`enEnsureHere`). Online: a friend's pose is shifted by whole loops (`EN_P`). No giant tree / porch yet. Test: `dev/test_endless.py` (seams, walk keeps up, revisit + loop give the same land, drop-in).
- **Endless regions (2.20):** the land is split into regions ~2.5 km across (`EN_RC`, warped Voronoi `enRegions` → up to 3 weights in `EN_RL`, blended over a wide border `EN_BL` = 900, ~800 m you can see changing; 2.21, was 380). Each region (`enRegInfo`, cached by wrapped cell) gets any of the 11 environments at random (or the menu's one if a specific environment is chosen), its own roughness (`set.rough` ± .5), its own weather (`weatherFor`, when the menu's weather is Random) and sky. Each environment uses its own typical latitude (`EN_LAT`) for the menu month. The per-environment rules now live in module-level `function* envGen(o)` (snow/tree lines, ground colours, tree kinds, `treeAt`); the island maps call it once (verified identical with same.py), endless makes a light one per environment at start (`E.envL`) and the heavy one (tree kinds, cards, limbs) the first time it is needed, a slice at a time, prefetched on rings ahead of you (`enPrepGen`, `E.prepQ`; tree kinds that look the same are shared via `E.kindCache`). In a square (`enMake` in en_make), each tree/rock/bush spot takes the environment of its spot (random by the blend weights near borders, so stands mix). Heights: `E.hNF` blends per-environment base heights (percentile-matched for each roughness, + swamp/desert/badlands touches), `E.hW` adds features. **Features:** one chance per 3.125 km square (`EN_FC`, `enFeat`: 85% with Dramatic, 40% with Natural), made by the island's `makeFeature` at the 3 km map's size (volcano, crater, canyon, butte, range, sink, terrace, rift…). Volcanoes get lava (`enLavaGen`, `enLavaAt`, `lavaAt`). As you walk `enAmbient` blends fog/light/sky/water colours and picks the rain/snow of the strongest wet region (strength = its share, `weather.pk`), and sets footsteps, sounds and the map's environment.
- **Endless 2.21:** **a day in 24 minutes** (`E.clock`, `hourNow` += dt/60 in `endlessTick` while playing, from the menu hour; `enLight` relights every 0.5 s; host sends `{t:"hr"}` every 4 s and in the start message, guests ease to it, `enClockSet`). **Tree shadows turn with the sun:** when it has moved ~5° (`enShadowSun`), the squares within 800 m (`EN_SHR`) are painted again into a second picture (`Sh.spare`/`Sh.nx`, `enShadowRebake`, `enShadowGen(ch, T)`), farther ones cleared, then it fades in (`uShadowMix`) and the two swap; no moon shadows on endless (they'd point the wrong way). **Far tree cards** are painted in a fixed light (`CARD_STD`) and tinted live (`CARD_TINT`, uniform `uCardTint` in `cardMaterial`), so they darken at dusk with the near trees. **Hand map** has its own small lane each frame (`E.mapJob`, 0.7 ms; recentres when you're 240 m off) — before, building new land starved it while walking. **Marks/bullets/arrows/grenades/lightning** use `outMap()` (no island edge on endless) — before, marks failed past 1.6 km from the start. **Leaving environments behind:** `enEnvGC` (in the 0.4 s scan) frees an environment unused for 60 s by any loaded square, the prep queue or the look-ahead ring (`enEnvFree`: slots, geometries, materials, textures, card cells → `E.sheet.free`); tree kinds shared with a live environment stay. **Env prep in smaller steps** (each atlas, odd/fluffy shape, bush, limb), and `enPrepGen` keeps the environment's own random stream across steps (before, shapes after the first step drew from the global stream, so they could differ between players). **Joining an endless hike online** puts you ~10 m from the host (before: back at the start). Tests: `test_endless` (map lag, far mark, clock, sun turns, same shapes), `test_en_online` (join near host, shared clock, split 3 km).
- **Endless animals (2.22, hiking):** the land is cut into 500 m squares (`EA_C`, 50 per loop). When a square comes within ~850 m of a player (`EA_ON`), its ground animals are planned from a seeded stream per wrapped square (`enAnimFill`: 3–5 groups, each kind picked from `NATIVE` of the environment where the group stands, rabbits weighted down; spots via `enAnimSpot` — water's edge for herons/gators/polar bears, high ground for goats/bighorn, shore for seals) and made a few per frame from `E.aQ` (≤1 ms; never within 700 m of anyone — they're out beyond the 750 m animal draw distance; dropping in fills behind the fade, `enEnsureHere` → `E.aInit`). Animals >1.25 km from everyone are taken away (`enAnimDrop`: rig meshes freed, shared model shapes kept); a square forgets itself once everyone is 1.6 km off, so it fills again later. Birds: one flock or lone bird per square ~3 in 4, made on each headset for itself (`E.acB`, `enBirdFill`). Coats follow the region (`addGround` uses the region's biome/season on endless). Online: endless counts like hunting for animals (`mpNetGuest`, `beastThreat`, `mpBeastSend`) — the host runs ground animals round every player and sends each friend the ones within 480 m. Same wandering/fleeing as hunting (`updateAnimals`, `appearTick`). No legends (Bigfoot/Yeti) or monkeys (need perch trees) on endless yet. Test: `dev/test_en_animals.py`; online part in `test_en_online`.

### Prehistoric (VR Hunting, `set.dino`)
- Herds of triceratops, parasaur, stego, bronto (`DINO_NATIVE`), sizes vary (`DINO_GROW`); health per kind (`DINO_HP`), damage per gun (`DINO_DMG`, `RAPTOR_DMG` × stars; head ×1.5); no health bar (Ron).

## 5. How to build and test

- **Edit with a Python patch script** in the scratchpad that replaces exact strings in a copy of `index.html`, **asserting each old string's count**; bump `VERSION` and `<title>`; build to the scratchpad, copy to `index.html` when tests pass.
- **Tools in `dev/`** (Playwright + Chromium/SwiftShader):
  - `check_code.py [file]`: parses, no duplicate function names, no debug hooks.
  - `make_debug.py index.html dbg.html`: debug copy with `window.FREEZE` (step frames by hand) and `window.D` (handles into the game; add more there when a test needs them).
  - `run_all.py <file> --nm /tmp/claude-0/-home-claude/93b90c2a-a1eb-59bd-91b2-a2c86d7f6262/scratchpad/nm/node_modules --jobs 5 [--only test_a,test_b]`: code check + tests. The full run takes ~25 min: run it in the background (`nohup … &`) and read the log. Online tests (test_mp, test_mp_fail, test_lobby, test_online) need that node_modules path (local PeerJS server).
  - Tests that load models serve the page over http (test_qdino, test_qanimals, test_bowtiers, test_rexsound); the rest open it from disk (old hand-built models).
  - Flaky under heavy parallel load, pass alone: test_dino (random raptor moods), test_habitat, test_spread, test_trex, test_bow. The model-loading tests (test_qdino, test_qanimals, test_bowtiers) and the big-map ones (test_landscape, test_solid_ground, test_cards) time out with `--jobs 5`: rerun them with `--jobs 1`–`2`.
- **Push:** `git add`, commit, `git push origin main`; then send the backup file.

### Gotchas (don't relearn these)
- In headless Chrome on this 2-core machine, endless walks show occasional 30–130 ms pauses even in trivial steps: garbage collection, not our steps (they were there in 2.19/2.20 too). If the Quest hitches, look at allocation churn (per-square Float32Arrays, `enBlock`, Colors in `setLighting`).
- Endless: anything scaled by height-over-water ÷ `span` must be capped — the big features stand many spans high (2.20: rock sections grew to 100 m). In picture scripts the sky dome only follows the camera in the live loop; move it yourself or you see a grey dome.
- Renderer outputs linear colour: **don't mark canvas textures as sRGB** or menus go dark. Hex colours in the model builders are sRGB → linear × `LIFT` (2.3).
- **Inside a VR session `requestAnimationFrame` doesn't run** — everything goes through `renderer.setAnimationLoop(loop)`.
- VR movement: `vr.readInputs()` gives world-space poses; walking moves the play space (`vr.ox/oy/oz/yaw`); `vr.placeAt` re-anchors on a new map. `VR_ON` must be set before `generate()`.
- `obstacles` is replaced by every `generate()`: use a getter in debug hooks.
- Two functions with the same name: the later silently wins (`check_code.py` catches it). A `//` comment in a one-line statement swallows the rest of the line — use `/* */` there.
- `vr.cl` is always an object in VR: test `vr.cl && vr.cl.hold` for "holding on".
- Call `updateMatrixWorld` before raycasts if things moved this frame.
- Quaternius farm rig: the feet are IK targets under the root, not children of the legs (`animal_lib` snaps them to the leg ends).
- A material's `onBeforeCompile` text isn't part of three's program cache key: anything that differs between materials sharing a hook must be a uniform, not baked into the shader text (`customProgramCacheKey` when the code differs).
- Island maps must stay identical when generation code is shared: compare a content fingerprint (trees, rocks, heights) old vs new with seeded `Math.random`, not pixels (rendering varies run to run).
- In the shell, `pkill -f` / `pgrep -f` can match the running shell and kill it; avoid them. Use the Bash timeout (up to 10 min) or nohup for long runs.
